\section{Results and Discussion}

Our results clearly show that temperature has a significant effect on liquid's viscosity.
Specifically, we found that as temperature increases, viscosity decreases in a non-linear
fashion as shown in \cite{jones2019}.

This finding is consistent with the hypothesis we laid out in the introduction. However,
there are a number of limitations to our study that must be acknowledged. First, our sample
size was quite small, which means that the results may not generalise to all situations.
Second, we were unable to account for all possible confounding variables.

Despite these limitations, we believe our findings make a valuable contribution to the
field. Future work should look into expanding the sample size and replicating these
results in more controlled environments.

\section{Conclusion}

In conclusion, this study has demonstrated that temperature significantly affects the
behaviour of liquids. The implications of these findings are broad and affect many areas
of science and engineering. We hope that this work will serve as a useful foundation
for future research in this important area.
